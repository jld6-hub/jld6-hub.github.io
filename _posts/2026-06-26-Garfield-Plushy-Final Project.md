My final project is my calming Garfield plushy. When deciding what to make for my final project, I was feeling a little overwhelmed with classes and my extracurriculars. I knew I wanted my project to involve some calming aspect. My mind immediately went to a Garfield plushy I had that always made me laugh, and I thought it would be a great idea to code the plushy to play a song when I give it a hug.

Comprehensive Materials List:
1 Lilypad Arduino
1 Lilypad Light Sensor
2 Lilypad white LEDs
1 Lilypad Buzzer
Conductive thread
Garfield Plushy
Thread, scissors, needle
